BUDGET GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK
1. Budget Approval Thresholds
   - <$50K: Department head approval only
   - $50K-$250K: VP approval + finance review
   - $250K-$1M: C-suite approval + board notification
   - >$1M: Full board approval required
2. Emergency Budget Authority
   - Crisis declaration allows 48-hour interim spend up to $100K
   - Must be ratified by CFO within 5 business days

STAKEHOLDER QUORUM RULES
1. Decision Quorum Requirements
   - Strategic decisions: 2/3 of named stakeholders must participate
   - Operational decisions: Simple majority (>50%)
   - Emergency decisions: Minimum 3 senior stakeholders
2. Consensus Thresholds
   - Unanimous consent required for: org restructuring, M&A, CEO succession
   - Supermajority (75%) for: budget reallocation >20%, new market entry
   - Simple majority for: hiring, vendor selection, process changes

CRISIS MANAGEMENT PROTOCOL
1. Crisis Classification
   - Level 1 (Monitor): Potential risk identified, no immediate action required
   - Level 2 (Respond): Active threat, response team assembled within 4 hours
   - Level 3 (Escalate): Material impact, CEO briefed within 1 hour, board within 24 hours
2. Communication Cadence
   - Level 1: Weekly status updates to stakeholders
   - Level 2: Daily briefings, external comms hold
   - Level 3: Continuous situation room, hourly updates, external PR activated
